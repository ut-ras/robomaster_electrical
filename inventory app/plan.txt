# Robotics team component inventory app

Build a mobile web app that works on iPhone and Android. We are starting from
an empty repo and a blank Google Sheet. We have existing Mouser bags and expect
new DigiKey bags. Both suppliers must work in the first release.

## 1. Scan a package

Let a teammate take a photo, upload one, or enter a part number manually.
Detect the supplier from the printed label and let the user correct it.
Use OCR to extract the supplier part number, manufacturer part number,
manufacturer name, description, and printed quantity when available.
Read recognized product barcodes where useful. Keep the photo visible during
review so the user can correct a character without retaking it.

Use both attached photos as scanner acceptance tests:

| Supplier | Supplier part number | Manufacturer | Manufacturer part number | Printed quantity |
|---|---|---|---|---:|
| Mouser | 771-74LVC244APW-T | Nexperia | 74LVC244APW,118 | 12 |
| DigiKey | WM20054-ND | Molex | 0387236506 | 2 |

Preserve the comma and suffix in the Nexperia number and the leading zero in
the Molex number through scanning, catalog lookup, saving, and searching.
Treat the online DigiKey photo as demo data.

## 2. Identify the part and retrieve its specs

Look up the supplier SKU using Mouser Search or DigiKey ProductDetails,
according to the bag. Cross-check the returned manufacturer and full part
number against the label. If the results disagree or several variants match,
show the choices for review. If the first catalog has gaps, use the second
catalog only when the manufacturer and full part number match exactly.

Save every technical attribute available for the matched part, including its
original name, value, unit, and source. Include product and datasheet links.
Show a short category-specific summary and let users expand “All specs.”

- Capacitors: capacitance, tolerance, rated voltage, dielectric, package, ESR.
- Resistors: resistance, tolerance, power rating, temperature coefficient.
- Inductors: inductance, tolerance, current ratings, DC resistance.
- ICs: function, supply voltage range, channels, pin count, package.
- Connectors and jumpers: positions, pitch, contact material, finish.

Preserve asymmetric tolerances such as −20%/+80%, absolute tolerances such
as ±0.1 pF, minimum and maximum ratings, and stated test conditions. Mark
missing specs as unknown. Allow manual entries with a datasheet page or source
link, and preserve verified edits when refreshing catalog data.

## 3. Save to one shared Google Sheet

Create tabs for Parts, Supplier SKUs, Specs, and Locations. Use one catalog
record per verified manufacturer and full manufacturer part number. Associate
it with any Mouser or DigiKey SKUs and all drawers where the team stores it.
Write part numbers as text.

On the review screen, let the member choose a drawer and confirm Save.
If both suppliers sold us the exact same component, searching either SKU
should open the same part and show its locations. If two components only
share electrical ratings, keep their catalog records separate. Make retried
saves safe. Keep credentials server-side and limit writes to team members.

## 4. Search the team's inventory

Search by either supplier SKU, manufacturer part number, description,
category, specs, or drawer. Show the location directly in the results.

Support searches like “100 nF capacitor, at least 25 V, ±10% or tighter.”
Treat 100 nF and 0.1 µF as equivalent. Test that a 50 V, ±5% part qualifies
and that a 16 V part, a ±20% part, and a part with unknown tolerance do not.

## 5. Add quantity tracking last

Track stock per part and drawer. When receiving stock, suggest the printed
quantity and ask the member to confirm the actual count. This matters for
old Mouser bags that may already be open. Record additions and removals with
member, time, bin, and quantity. Handle duplicate submissions and concurrent
removals safely. Scanning a bag by itself must never change stock.

## First delivery

Use a single mobile-friendly TypeScript project. Build the smallest complete
demo: scan each sample photo, review the exact part and its catalog specs,
save both to different drawers in a test Google Sheet, then find both from
a second phone. Also test a capacitor by manual part-number entry to verify
tolerance, voltage, and capacitance search. Document setup, credentials, and
which checks used live APIs versus demo data.
